I'm Matěj Kadlec and I'm a freelance developer

I'm a 25-year-old developer from Opava, Czechia, with more than five years of experience. I studied computer science at VSB – Technical University of Ostrava, and I currently work remotely as a full-stack developer at DHL IT Services, focusing on Python and Django.

Over the years, I have worked in many areas of IT – as an IT article editor, QA engineer, web developer, back-end developer, and ETL developer. You can find all the technologies I have experience with in my CV.

I also take on freelance work and personal side projects, such as Python web and desktop applications, AutoHotkey automation scripts, and World of Warcraft add-ons. Visit my GitHub to see them – most are MIT-licensed, and collaborators are always welcome!
